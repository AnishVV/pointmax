## Why this matters for the tool

New routes launch with promotional intro fares and frequently have thin award availability for the first 6–12 months (not enough history for partners to release saver-level seats). Flag brand-new routes as a distinct, temporarily-favorable cash-fare category, while expecting weak award inventory on them.

## Broader 2026 capacity pattern

* Gulf carriers are in an active capacity race — Qatar Airways alone pushed past 150–170 destinations in 2026 network expansions; Emirates, Etihad, and Turkish Airlines are adding frequencies in parallel. More Gulf capacity generally softens cash pricing on Europe/US ↔ South Asia/Africa routings through DXB/DOH/AUH/IST.
* India is the most contested long-haul market right now — Delta, Emirates, Air India, Qatar Airways, British Airways, and Singapore Airlines have all announced new or expanded India routes in 2026 — good news for US/Europe ↔ India pricing.
