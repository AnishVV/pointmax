# Hub Airports, Alliances & Low-Cost Carriers

> Imported 2026-10-03 from the "Travel point maximizer" project (pasted by Anish into the "Import artifacts from earlier project" thread). Text verbatim; tab-separated tables converted to markdown tables.

**Volatility: MEDIUM** — alliance rosters and hub assignments shift occasionally (e.g. ITA Airways joined Star Alliance in April 2026). Re-verify before hardcoding partner/hub logic.

## Global alliances

| Alliance | Anchor carrier | Other major members | Best use case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Star Alliance | United | Lufthansa, Air Canada, Singapore Airlines, ANA, Air India, Turkish Airlines, ITA Airways (joined 2026) | Broadest network (~1,150+ destinations); strong Europe/Asia via Frankfurt, Munich, Istanbul, Singapore |
| Oneworld | American | British Airways, Qatar Airways, Cathay Pacific, Japan Airlines, Qantas, Hawaiian (post-Alaska merger) | Best long-haul business sweet spots (BA Avios short-haul, Qatar Qsuite via Doha) |
| SkyTeam | Delta | Air France, KLM, Korean Air, Virgin Atlantic, SAS | Strong Europe (Paris/Amsterdam) and Korea/Asia; Flying Blue is a favorite transfer target |

Independent (no alliance): Emirates, Etihad, JetBlue (has Oneworld-adjacent partnerships), most ultra-low-cost carriers. Still bookable one-way via transferable points (Amex/Chase/Capital One) without an alliance tie.

| Hub | Carrier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai (DXB) | Emirates | Independent; huge network, competitive fares to South Asia/Africa |
| Doha (DOH) | Qatar Airways | Oneworld; top-rated transit airport, strong business value |

These four collectively are the cheapest way to bridge Europe/US ↔ South Asia, Africa, Southeast Asia — worth pricing as a connection even off the obvious path.

## Low-cost carriers (for positioning legs)

Budget carriers rarely interline with full-service airlines — always book as a separate ticket with a generous buffer, never as one connected itinerary.

| Region | Key carriers | Typical bases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Europe | Ryanair, easyJet, Wizz Air, Vueling | Dublin, London-Stansted/Luton, Milan-Bergamo, Budapest, Barcelona — often €20–50 one-way |
| US domestic | Spirit, Frontier, Allegiant, Southwest | Fort Lauderdale, Denver, Las Vegas, Orlando |
| Asia | AirAsia, Scoot, IndiGo, VietJet | Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Delhi, Ho Chi Minh City |
| Latin America | Volaris, JetSMART, Viva Aerobus | Mexico City, Santiago, Guadalajara |
| Long-haul budget | Norse Atlantic, French Bee, Icelandair (via KEF), Condor | NYC–London/Paris, LAX–Paris, transatlantic via Reykjavik |

Tactic: a $50–150 domestic/short-haul budget leg to a major gateway frequently unlocks a cheaper transatlantic cash fare or a lower-mileage award that only prices well from that gateway.
